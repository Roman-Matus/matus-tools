# Cast Maker — release notes

## 1.0.0 — first release

- Cast parts from primitives: fillets where parts meet, rounded edges, casting grain; Cut Shape and Cut Metal cuts;
  straight and bent pipes with bare or painted ends and inner walls.
- Surface: paint, bare metal, rust, dirt; edge wear, chips, rust streaks, scratches, metal age; 8 built-in presets
  and your own.
- Prototype Library: save a cast and add it to any project; three samples included (Angle Valve, Flange Pipe, Handwheel).
- Built-in game asset pipeline: Apply Cast, Make Low Poly (with Suggest, Keep Sharp Edges, Cap Holes), Bake Textures,
  Export Low Poly for Unreal Engine, Unity, Godot.
- Blender 5.0, 5.1 and 5.2.
